# DQN Pendulum Control

A group coursework project studying Deep Q-Network control of a pendulum under four gravity settings: default gravity, free fall, anti-gravity and supergravity.

## My contribution

The contribution statement records my primary responsibility as the implementation and training lead. I implemented the torque-action discretisation, Q-network, replay memory, exploration strategy and target-network training. I also ran configuration experiments and final training across the four settings, and prepared the reproducible training setup and saved model weights.

Evaluation, visualisation and interpretation were led by my teammate. We jointly discussed the methodology and reviewed the final work. Workload was split evenly.

## Techniques

- Discretised a continuous torque action space for DQN
- Used replay memory and a target network during training
- Compared training configurations across distinct gravity conditions
- Saved model weights for later evaluation
